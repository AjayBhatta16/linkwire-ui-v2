About Us

LinkWire is a URL shortening and web traffic analytics platform that empowers cybersecurity professionals to identify and assess threat actors across the internet. With our shortened URLs, you can get an inconspicuous link that will redirect users in the blink of an eye, taking them to a destination of your choice.

With our dashboard, you can view real-time access logs for your URLs and discover a wide variety of information on the users who clicked it.

Powered by the latest technology from Google Cloud, we deliver a seamless experience to both the sender and the clicker. This is why The Baitcave and many others consider us their #1 choice for all of their web traffic analysis needs.

In addition to our URL service, we offer a suite of free analytics tools for parsing user agents, expanding URLs, and more. These are accessible free of charge with no sign-in required.
